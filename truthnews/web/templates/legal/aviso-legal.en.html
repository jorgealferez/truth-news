What this site is

{{ site.site_domain }} is a digital news medium that compares what the media publish with what public data say (INE, Eurostat, World Bank, BOE and other official sources). It is free and needs no registration.

Intellectual property and quotations

The analysis, our own texts and the design belong to {{ site.site_domain }}. Headlines and excerpts from other outlets are briefly quoted, linked to the original, for analysis and criticism (article 32 of the Spanish Intellectual Property Law). We respect outlets' text and data mining opt-outs (article 4 of Directive (EU) 2019/790): we do not download pages that forbid it in their robots.txt. Official data are reused under Law 37/2007 and each source's licence, cited next to each figure. The source code is public under CC0 1.0.

Right of reply and correction
