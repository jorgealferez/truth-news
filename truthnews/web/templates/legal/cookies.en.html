Why there is no cookie banner

Article 22(2) LSSI and Directive 2002/58/EC only require your consent to store or read information on your device when it is not strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Everything in the table is of that kind (Spanish Data Protection Agency cookie guide, 2023). We use no analytics, advertising, social media or third-party cookies, and ads are chosen by the page topic, without profiles. If we ever add something that needs consent, we will ask first, making it as easy to refuse as to accept.

Ads with an image load it from the advertiser's domain, without telling it which page you are on.
