Editorial independence

No outlet, party, administration or advertiser pays to appear, for its score or for a verdict. Advertising never influences what we publish (advertising) and the method is open (methodology).

AI-generated content

Some texts (summaries, analysis and translations) are generated with artificial intelligence from the cited evidence. They are always marked as AI-generated, as article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) requires, and link their sources. If you spot an error, ask for a correction.

Corrections

We fix errors on the page itself and say so. Corrections and updates of old stories follow the procedure in the legal notice.

Accessibility

We work to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA (EN 301 549): keyboard navigation, skip-to-content link, sufficient contrast, text alternatives and working without JavaScript.
